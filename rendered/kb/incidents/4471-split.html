How each report was placed

  1. One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Each is counted once.
  2. One-day moves. Some reports say the date moved by one day: Tuesday became Monday, a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto got different days for the same meeting, every June booking is a day off. A flipped month cannot do that. The desk had already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). Here they are their own problem with their own ticket. They stay counted under the cause they are filed under, as the desk counts.
  3. The rest by tenant and channel (causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first. A report through a booking-v3 form is the hint problem: the form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY and then reads the date the tenant’s way. Every other report is filed under the parser problem: the shared parser reads the date month first.
  4. Not by intake tag. Intake guesses the cause from the reporter’s browser locale. The “intake tagged as” column shows how often that guess is wrong.

The report bodies back this up. Every body that names two dates names the same day and month, swapped, except the one-day moves. A few bodies name no date at all (“is there a setting for my country writes the day first”, “our October bookings came through for January”). They stay with their tenant’s cause.

Why it was one ticket

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. “Wrong date again” reads the same whether the parser flipped the date, the form hint misled the person, or the date moved by a day. The three problems share subjects, so they shared a ticket.

What changes for the desk

  • Closing #4471 closed the parser and hint problems only. Keep the shift ticket open until it has a fix of its own.
  • When a report comes in, place it with causeOf and the tenant, not by subject or intake tag.
  • Read the body for a one-day move before merging it into a month-flip ticket.
